ABOUT EXTREMO OESTE

Begin your journey into Lima with us.
Experience more than tourism, step right into living culture.

Lima is the main gateway to Peru. For a long time, it was underrated because of more famous destinations like Cusco or Nazca. Although Lima is considered a modern city, beneath its urban landscape lie over fifteen hundred years of continuous human settlement and city-building. The present-day city is the result of the many cultures and bloodlines that have shaped Peru over thousands of years of human life in this region. Much of this history was made possible by the sea, the great provider of life in the middle of the coastal desert.

EXTREMO OESTE creates alternative experiences in Lima focused on history, urban life, ecology, and human connection. Our routes are designed for travelers who want to understand Lima beyond tourism.
